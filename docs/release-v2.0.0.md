# SilkStack Image Browser v2.0.0

The next release of SilkStack Image Browser is here, packed with hundreds of improvements across the board. The headline feature in v2.0.0 is **Smart Stacking** — a powerful new way to organize your AI-generated images.

### Smart Stacking (Premium Feature)

Tired of scrolling through endless variations of the same prompt? Smart Stacking brings order to your library by grouping related images together:

- **Automatic Stacking by Similarity** — Images generated from similar prompts are automatically grouped into stacks, keeping variations neatly bundled without cluttering your grid.
- **Flexible Grouping** — Choose how stacks are formed by combining grouping parameters your way: group by **Prompt**, **Model**, **Lora**, or any combination of them together.
- **Dedicated Stack View** — A brand-new view designed specifically for browsing stacked images, making it effortless to navigate and compare variations within each group.
- **Manual Stack & Unstack** — Take full control: drag images together to create custom stacks, or pull individual images out of a stack when they don't belong.
- **Merge Stacks** — Combine multiple stacks into one, making it easy to consolidate related groups you want to keep together.

### More Improvements

Beyond stacking, this release is packed with enhancements across the entire app:

- **Aspect Ratio Filters** — Quickly narrow your library by **Portrait**, **Landscape**, or **Square** orientation to find exactly the composition you need.
- **Folder Management** — Right-click to manage folders directly in the app: rename, move, and organize your collections without leaving your workspace.
- **ComfyUI JSON Parser** — Improved parsing of ComfyUI workflow metadata, giving you richer insight into your generation parameters.
- **WebP Metadata Support** — Full metadata processing for compressed WebP images — no file format gets left behind.
- **Native Image Viewer** — Open any image in your system's native viewer with a single click for a closer look.
- **Enhanced List View** — More useful columns added to the list view, surfacing richer data at a glance.
- **Performance & Polish** — Hundreds of smaller refinements: UI polish, performance optimizations, and quality-of-life improvements throughout.

### Premium Features

Unlock the full potential of your AI image library with these premium-tier capabilities:

- **Image Stacking** — All Smart Stacking functionality described above: automatic grouping by prompt similarity, flexible multi-parameter grouping, the dedicated Stack View, and full manual stack/unstack control.
- **AI-Based Auto-Tagging** — Let the app intelligently analyze and tag your images using AI. Say goodbye to manual tagging — the system automatically detects subjects, styles, and content to keep your library searchable with zero effort.
- **More AI Features Coming Soon** — We're just getting started. Stay tuned for even more AI-powered tools to supercharge your workflow in upcoming releases.
